## Open questions for the user

- The catalogue names no Angular component for loan-calculator and currency-converter, so they are not built in Angular (its `CalcBase` would make them three lines each).
- The calculators use the standard formulas: the instalment of an annuity loan, deposit interest compounded quarterly (compounding sets it), and amount times rate for an exchange. They say they are estimates. If the bank works out interest another way (daily, simple, with fees), the screen should show the service's figure instead: say if the calculators should take a result from the screen.
- eligibility-calculator and goal-calculator are a slider for the customer's figure; the result line (how much they could borrow, when they reach the goal) is the screen's, in helpText, because it needs the bank's rules.
