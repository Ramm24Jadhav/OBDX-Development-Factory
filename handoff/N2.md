### Props worth a note (for the catalogue's `api` blocks)

- Charts (BarChart, SpendAnalysisWidget, PendingActionWidget): `bars: {label, value}[]`, `currency`, `value`, `onChange`.
- Progress (ProgressBar, TransactionLimits, GoalsWidget, BudgetsWidget): `items: {label, value, max}[]`, `currency`,
  `measure: 'used' | 'saved' | 'score'`. SavingsGoal: `saved`, `target`, `currency`, `onAdd`. CreditScoreWidget:
  `score`, `max`, `band` (the bureau's word; the component does not grade), `updated`.
- ApprovalQueue / PendingApprovalsWidget: `items` (`priority` sorts first), `onApprove`, `onReject`, `onSelect`.
  ApprovalDetail: `rows`, `onApprove`, `onReject(reason)`, `onSendBack(reason)`; a reason is required.
- Devices: `devices` (`current` gets no Remove), `onRemove`. Consents: `onRevoke`. LanguagePicker: `languages`
  (`{value: code, label: own-language name}`).
- Calculators take the rates from the screen: LoanCalculator, DepositCalculator `value: {amount, rate, months}`;
  ForexCalculator `value` (sent), `currency`, `to`, `rate`; EligibilityCalculator `annualRate`, `months`, `share` (40%
  of income by default); GoalCalculator `target`. Each result says it is an estimate.
- TermsConsent: `options`, `mustAccept` (ids), `onContinue`. FeedbackRating: value 1–5, `comment`, `onSubmit`.
- Locator: `places: {id, name, kind: 'atm'|'branch', address, distance, hours}[]`, `onDirections`, `map` (the app's
  map component; the pack has no maps dependency). LiveChat: `messages: {id, from: 'customer'|'bank', text, time}[]`,
  `suggestions`, `onSend`, `typing`.
- AccountsWidget / TransactionsWidget: `tabs: {id, label, summary}[]`, `value`, `onChange`, `onOpen`.
  DelinquencyNotice: `overdue` (renders nothing when false).

## Open questions for the user

- The calculators use standard formulas (reducing-balance instalment, quarterly compounding, 40% of income for
  eligibility) and label every result an estimate. If the bank uses other conventions (monthly compounding, a
  different income share), they are props, but the defaults are a choice to confirm.
